## 3. Data the system can build on

### Complaint ↔ transaction matching experiment

`complaints` has no `transaction_id` and `affected_product_id` never belongs to the complainant, so each dispute complaint with an amount (8,957 of 27,133) is matched against the complainant's own transactions from the 60 days before it was filed. The control repeats the search with each claim assigned to a random other complainant: if amounts pointed to real transactions, the true customer would match far more often.

| Amount tolerance | Currency | True customer: 0 / 1 / 2+ candidates | Random customer (control): 0 / 1 / 2+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ±1% | same | 99.9% / 0.1% / 0.0% | 99.9% / 0.1% / 0.0% |
| ±1% | any | 99.6% / 0.4% / 0.0% | 99.6% / 0.4% / 0.0% |
| ±5% | same | 99.5% / 0.5% / 0.0% | 99.6% / 0.4% / 0.0% |
| ±5% | any | 98.2% / 1.8% / 0.0% | 98.4% / 1.6% / 0.0% |

Why: `claimed_amount` is spread evenly between 51 and 5,000 (median 2,515) whatever its currency, while the median transaction is ARS 163,285, COP 1,867,136, USD 467. Claim currencies (USD 2,166, MXN 2,145, COP 2,112, ARS 2,100, (none) 434) are unrelated to the customer's country, and MXN claims exist although no transaction is in MXN. 68.3% of claimants have any transaction in the window (median 1). **The complaint amounts are synthetic noise: complaints tell us how many disputes there are and how they are handled, not which transaction they are about.**

### Transactions, cards and labels

- 4,425,008 transactions, 1,547,432 on cards (Tarjeta Crédito 100,102, Tarjeta Débito 39,938 card products) from 82,138 customers; types: Purchase 1,083,406, Withdrawal 232,392, Payment 231,634.
- `is_fraud` marks 1,548 card transactions (0.10%): usable as an evaluation label for unrecognized-charge cases, never as an input.
- No two card transactions share merchant and amount within 2 days (quality report): no organic duplicate charges.

### Natural-language data

- `call_transcripts`: 171,321 transcripts, but only **42 distinct customer texts** and 546 distinct full texts, 1 intent value(s) and 1 language(s) (es 171,321). They open with: "Hola, buenos días. Quisiera saber cuál es mi saldo actual en mi cuenta de ahorros." (85,411); "Buenas tardes, necesito consultar el saldo de mi tarjeta de crédito." (51,555). 0 transcripts mention a charge, fraud, ATM, theft, block or refund.
- `complaints.description`: 5 distinct texts for 67,095 complaints ("Queja relacionada con …").
- `complaints.origin_interaction_id` is set in 0 complaints: complaints cannot be linked to their call or transcript.
- No Portuguese anywhere (the dataset covers Mexico, Colombia and Argentina).

## What this means for the team

1. **The flow stands** (volume and pain are there) and the case for it is service time and SLA: disputes are 40% of complaints and take about two weeks each today.
2. **P3 — classifier and prompts:** the dataset has no dispute utterances in any language. Training and dev data must be `team_generated` (ES and PT), labeled with the C6 taxonomy; the transcripts only serve as out-of-scope examples (balance questions).
3. **P4 — evaluation:** held-out cases are `team_generated` utterances on the demo customers' real transactions; `duplicate_charge` needs fixture transactions; `is_fraud` can back the expected outcome of unrecognized-charge cases. Baseline numbers for the pitch come from the tables above.
4. **Transaction lookup** stays as the backend does it: search the customer's own card transactions by amount, date and merchant from the conversation. Complaints cannot seed it.
5. **Taxonomy C6 draft** in [`analysis/mappings/contact_reason_to_flow.csv`](../analysis/mappings/contact_reason_to_flow.csv): `Transactions` → `unrecognized_charge`, `Fees` → `other_in_scope` (or `wrong_amount`, to agree with P3/P4), other categories → `out_of_scope`; `Queja`/`Transaccional` contacts are mixed and cannot be mapped.
