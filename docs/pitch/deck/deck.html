What · the product

Four things, done right.

01 · FIND

The exact charge, from a vague sentence

Searches only the customer's own cards. Shows the amount in their currency, in USD and the day's rate.

02 · EXPLAIN

Not every scary charge is a dispute

Reversed, pending or declined: it explains from the record instead of opening a case nobody needs.

03 · ACT & VERIFY

A dispute that really exists

Acts only after the customer confirms, then reads the case back before saying it's done. Card blocks need a second factor.

04 · ESCALATE

A specialist gets the whole case

Verified facts, actions taken and open questions. The name stays encrypted until the case is claimed.

The LLM never executes

It understands and words; deterministic code decides with a policy the bank can read and audit.

2
Explicit consent, verified writes

Only two actions exist, each needs a confirmation; blocking a card also needs TOTP. Nothing is "done" until read back.

3
Privacy by construction

Opaque customer reference, RLS per customer, AES-GCM names revealed only to the claiming agent and audited.

Replies come from reviewed templates filled with verified data; the model sees masked text only.

Observable and measurable

Every turn traced, every decision tagged with its rule; a built-in baseline mode runs today's process on the same input.

Data · models · evaluation

Built on the data, measured honestly.

Data engineering

Reproducible raw → silver

  • Declarative specs drive DuckDB stages and Pandera contracts
  • Bad rows go to quarantine with a rule id, never silently dropped
  • Lineage, watermarks and late-arrival rebuilds; idempotent re-runs
  • Business dates from event timestamps; USD recomputed at the day's rate
Machine learning

Small models that know when to stop

  • Classifier: multilingual-e5 + TF-IDF ensemble for 8 dispute types
  • Abstains when unsure (≥ 0.95 precision when it acts); the assistant asks instead
  • DeepSeek Flash extracts amount, date and merchant and screens manipulation
  • No leakage: fraud and resolution labels are never inputs
Evaluation

Same input, two systems

  • 120 held-out cases (ES/PT), frozen by hash, each tied to a real transaction
  • Normal, ambiguous, human-required, non-final and adversarial
  • Baseline = today's process: every dispute goes to a human
  • Wilson 95% intervals, p50/p95 latency and cost per case

Honest by design: our first case set was written by the same team that wrote the training phrases and overstated accuracy. We wrote and froze a second set afterwards, and that is the number we report.

  • Country-specific legal rules in the policy, reviewed by compliance
  • A data-processing agreement or a self-hosted model for the LLM
